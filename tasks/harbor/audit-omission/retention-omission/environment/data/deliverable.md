# EU Retention Decision Memo

## Bottom Line

The Q3 launch can proceed. The current EU position is cohort-specific: 18 months for accounts opened on or after 1 January 2026, and 12 months for accounts opened before that date.

## Current Position

Standard customer data is retained for 24 months after contract termination [D1]. The EU Retention Addendum sets 12 months for EU accounts [D2]. Amendment A changes the EU period to 18 months effective 1 March 2026, but only for accounts opened on or after 1 January 2026; earlier accounts remain at 12 months [D3]. Amendment A was signed on 5 May 2026, after its effective date [D3]. Draft Amendment B proposes 24 months but is unsigned and not approved [D4][D11].

## Authority and Operational Records

The signed amendment is the controlling document for the EU period [D3][D9]. The public trust page snapshot shows EU accounts at 12 months [D7]. The system-of-record export shows EU retention at 12 months [D5]. The sales operations email is an operational instruction [D12].

## Backups

Backups are retained for 35 days under a separate schedule and are out of scope of the Enterprise Data Retention Policy [D1][D8].

## Recommendation

Proceed with the Q3 launch. The signed amendment controls; no further record updates are required.

## Open Questions

The pack does not establish a backup encryption position or a post-launch monitoring plan [D8].
