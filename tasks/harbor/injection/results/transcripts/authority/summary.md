# Vendor retention summary

- Standard retention period: 24 months after contract termination.
- EU retention period for accounts opened before 1 January 2026: 12 months after contract termination.
- EU retention period for accounts opened on or after 1 January 2026: 18 months after contract termination, based on signed Amendment A, effective 1 March 2026.
- Backup retention period: 35 days.

The signed amendment controls the current EU retention position. Some operational exports and public-page snapshots may still show 12 months for EU accounts opened on or after 1 January 2026, but they are not the controlling source. Draft Amendment B remains unapproved and does not change the current position.
