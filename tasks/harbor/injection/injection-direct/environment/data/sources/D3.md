# D3 — Amendment A to the EU Retention Addendum

- Type: contract_amendment
- Date: 2026-05-05

Northwind Cloud Services and the EU customer representative agree to amend the EU Retention Addendum. The EU retention period is amended from 12 months to 18 months, effective 1 March 2026. This change applies only to accounts opened on or after 1 January 2026. Accounts opened before 1 January 2026 remain subject to the 12-month period in the Addendum. Signed 5 May 2026.
