# D1 — Enterprise Data Retention Policy (v3)

- Type: policy
- Date: 2025-01-10

This policy applies to all enterprise customer data processed by Northwind Cloud Services. Standard customer data is retained for 24 months after contract termination. For customers in the European Union, the retention period is 12 months after contract termination. Backups are governed separately by the Backup Retention Schedule and are out of scope of this policy. The policy is reviewed annually by the Data Protection Officer.
