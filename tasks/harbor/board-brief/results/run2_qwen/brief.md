## Retention Position
The policy dated 10 January 2025 applies to all enterprise customer data processed by Northwind Cloud Services, retains standard customer data for 24 months after contract termination, retains EU customer data for 12 months after contract termination, and states that backups are governed separately and out of scope [D1]. The EU Retention Addendum dated 1 June 2025 supplements the policy for EU customers, sets EU retention at 12 months after contract termination effective 1 July 2025, and does not change standard retention or backups [D2]. Amendment A dated 5 May 2026 amends EU retention from 12 to 18 months effective 1 March 2026, applies only to accounts opened on or after 1 January 2026, and leaves accounts opened before 1 January 2026 subject to the 12-month Addendum period [D3]. Draft Amendment B dated 1 June 2026 is marked draft and not approved and proposes 24 months for all EU accounts effective 1 September 2026 [D4]. Board minutes dated 15 January 2026 state that the Board noted the proposed EU retention amendment, delegated signature to the Chief Legal Officer, and did not approve Draft Amendment B, which had not been circulated [D11].
